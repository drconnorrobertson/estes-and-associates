Meet Kari Estes

Kari Estes founded Estes & Associates with a simple mission: to bring world-class accounting and tax resolution expertise to businesses and individuals across Central Missouri. With over 30 years of experience in tax preparation, accounting, and IRS problem resolution, Kari has built a reputation for integrity, expertise, and unwavering commitment to client success.

In 2022, Kari's dedication and impact were recognized when she received the prestigious "Women Who Own It" Award from the Missouri Women's Business Center, cementing her place as one of Missouri's most respected accounting professionals.

Our Story

Founded over 30 years ago, Estes & Associates has grown from a single office to five strategically located branches across Central Missouri. This expansion wasn't built on aggressive marketing or aggressive tactics, but on one principle: deliver exceptional results for every client, every time.

What started as a small accounting practice has evolved into a full-service firm offering comprehensive solutions in tax preparation, accounting, bookkeeping, payroll processing, and specialized IRS resolution services. This growth reflects our clients' trust and the quality of work our team delivers daily.

Our recent relocation to our new headquarters at 3401 W. Truman Blvd Suite 201 in Jefferson City marks an exciting new chapter while staying true to our core values of client-first service and technical excellence.
