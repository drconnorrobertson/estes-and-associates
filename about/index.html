Meet Kari Estes

Kari Estes founded Estes & Associates with a simple mission: to bring world-class tax planning, accounting, and financial advisory expertise to businesses and individuals nationwide. With over 30 years of experience in tax preparation, accounting, tax planning, and strategic financial guidance, Kari has built a reputation for integrity, expertise, and unwavering commitment to client success.

In 2022, Kari's dedication and impact were recognized when she received the prestigious "Women Who Own It" Award from the Missouri Women's Business Center, cementing her place as one of Missouri's most respected accounting professionals.

Our Story

Founded over 30 years ago, Estes & Associates has grown from a single office in Missouri to a nationally-recognized firm serving clients across all 50 states. This expansion wasn't built on aggressive marketing or aggressive tactics, but on one principle: deliver exceptional results for every client, every time.

What started as a small accounting practice has evolved into a full-service firm offering comprehensive solutions in tax planning, tax preparation, accounting, bookkeeping, payroll processing, financial statements, and business advisory services. When clients face IRS challenges, we also bring specialized resolution expertise. Our Missouri heritage combined with our national reach allows us to serve diverse clients with deep, local expertise and proactive tax strategies. This growth reflects our clients' trust and the quality of work our team delivers daily.

Our recent relocation to our new headquarters at 3401 W. Truman Blvd Suite 201 in Jefferson City marks an exciting new chapter while staying true to our core values of client-first service and technical excellence.
