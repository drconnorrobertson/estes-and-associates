Why Back Taxes Continue Growing

Many people dont realize that simply not paying taxes doesnt make the debt go away. In fact, it gets worse every single day. Heres what happens financially:

  • Interest: The IRS charges interest at the federal rate plus 3 percent, compounded daily. This means your debt grows dramatically over time.
  • Penalties: The IRS adds penalties for failing to file and failing to pay. These penalties can reach 75 percent of your original tax liability.
  • Accumulation: A 5,000 dollar debt from five years ago might now be 10,000 dollars or more with interest and penalties.

The Missouri-Specific Angle

Missouri has its own tax system separate from federal taxes. If you owe federal taxes, you likely owe Missouri state taxes as well. Both must be addressed in any resolution strategy. Missouri imposes its own penalties and interest, further complicating your situation.

Common Reasons for Back Taxes in Missouri

Understanding why you have back taxes helps you avoid the problem in the future:

  • Business owners: Changes in business structure or cash flow can catch business owners off guard at tax time.
  • Self-employed individuals: Without employer withholding, self-employed people must pay estimated quarterly taxes or face significant bills.
  • Multiple income sources: When you have W-2 income plus rental income, investment income, or business income, tax withholding becomes complicated.
  • Life changes: Divorce, death in the family, or major financial changes can disrupt tax planning.
  • Avoidance: Sometimes people simply avoid filing returns because they know theyll owe, hoping the problem will go away on its own.
