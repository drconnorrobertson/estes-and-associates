Join Estes & Associates

Estes & Associates is a growing firm dedicated to providing exceptional tax, accounting, and financial services to Missouri businesses and individuals. We're looking for talented professionals to join our team.

Why Work With Us?

We offer more than just a job. We offer a career with:

  • Competitive compensation and benefits
  • Professional development and training opportunities
  • Collaborative team environment
  • Work-life balance
  • Chance to help real people and businesses succeed
  • Growth potential as the firm expands
  • Latest technology and tools

Our Culture

At Estes & Associates, we value:

  • Excellence in everything we do
  • Integrity and honesty in client relationships
  • Continuous learning and improvement
  • Teamwork and collaboration
  • Client success and satisfaction
  • Community involvement

Available Positions

We regularly have openings for:

  • Tax Preparers and Tax Specialists
  • Bookkeepers and Accounting Clerks
  • CPAs and Accounting Managers
  • Administrative and Support Staff
  • Client Service Representatives

What We're Looking For

Ideal candidates have:

  • Strong accounting, tax, or bookkeeping skills
  • Attention to detail and accuracy
  • Commitment to professional excellence
  • Ability to manage multiple projects
  • Strong communication skills
  • Customer service orientation
  • Problem-solving ability
  • Willingness to learn and grow
