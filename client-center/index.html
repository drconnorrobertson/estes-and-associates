Secure Client Portal

Estes & Associates uses a secure client portal for document sharing and communication. Our portal is protected with encryption and security measures to keep your sensitive tax and financial information safe.

What You Can Do in the Portal

  • Upload tax documents and financial records

Document Security

Your documents are among your most sensitive possessions. We take security seriously. Our client portal uses:

  • 256-bit SSL encryption for all communications
  • Secure password protection and two-factor authentication
  • Regular security audits and compliance with data protection standards
  • Automatic session timeouts to prevent unauthorized access
