Comprehensive Corporate Tax Return Services

Corporate tax returns require precision, expertise, and knowledge of current tax law. Whether you operate as a C-Corporation or S-Corporation, Estes & Associates provides comprehensive tax return preparation that ensures compliance while identifying legitimate tax-saving opportunities.

Our corporate tax return services have helped hundreds of Missouri businesses minimize their tax liability while maintaining complete IRS compliance. We understand the complexities of corporate tax reporting and stay current on the latest tax law changes that affect your bottom line.

C-Corporation Tax Returns

C-Corporations face unique tax situations. We prepare complete Form 1120 corporate tax returns including:

  • Corporate income tax calculations and estimated tax planning
  • Depreciation schedules and asset management
  • Dividend income and intercompany transactions
  • Business expense optimization and deduction maximization
  • State and local corporate tax compliance
  • Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) calculations when required

S-Corporation Tax Returns

S-Corporations offer tax advantages but require careful tax planning. Our S-Corp tax return services include:

  • Form 1120-S return preparation with K-1 schedules
  • Reasonable salary and distribution planning for tax efficiency
  • Built-in gains tax and passive income calculations
  • Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction optimization
  • Basis tracking and shareholder equity analysis
  • Multi-state S-Corp tax filing strategies
