Protecting Your Finances from Fraud

Tax fraud and identity theft threaten your financial security. Understanding fraud risks and taking protective steps helps keep your finances and tax information safe.

Types of Tax Fraud

  • Identity Theft: Criminals using your SSN to file fraudulent returns
  • W-2 Fraud: Hackers gaining access to employer systems
  • Phishing Scams: Fraudulent emails impersonating the IRS
  • Refund Fraud: Filing false returns to claim refunds
  • Document Fraud: Creating false tax documents

Protecting Yourself

  • Monitor your credit reports regularly
  • Use strong, unique passwords for accounts
  • Be cautious of unsolicited communications
  • Verify IRS communications before responding
  • Keep tax documents in a secure location
  • File your return early to prevent fraud filing
  • Consider an IP PIN from the IRS
