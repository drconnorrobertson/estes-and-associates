Professional Individual Tax Preparation

Individual tax preparation is our foundation. For over 30 years, we've helped thousands of Missouri residents prepare accurate, optimized tax returns that maximize refunds and minimize tax liability. Whether your situation is simple or complex, our experienced tax professionals ensure every detail is handled correctly.

We believe tax preparation should be straightforward. You focus on your business and family while we handle the tax details, ensuring you get every deduction and credit you're entitled to.

What We Handle

  • W-2 income from employment
  • Self-employment and business income (Schedule C)
  • Rental property income (Schedule E)
  • Investment income and capital gains
  • Retirement account contributions and distributions
  • Education expenses and credits
  • Mortgage interest deductions
  • Charitable contributions
  • Medical and dental expenses
  • State and local tax deductions
  • Home office deductions for self-employed individuals
  • Business vehicle expenses and depreciation

Tax Deductions for Individuals

Many taxpayers miss valuable deductions. Here are deductions we help our individual clients maximize:

  • Self-Employment Expenses: Business vehicle, equipment, supplies, utilities
  • Home Office Deduction: Both simplified and actual expense methods
  • Retirement Contributions: Traditional and Roth IRA, Solo 401k
  • Education Credits: American Opportunity, Lifetime Learning, AOTC
  • Charitable Contributions: Donations to qualifying organizations
  • Investment Losses: Capital losses for tax reduction

Our Individual Tax Preparation Process

  1. Initial Consultation: We meet to understand your tax situation and changes from last year
  2. Document Collection: You provide all necessary tax documents (W-2s, 1099s, receipts)
  3. Comprehensive Review: Our team identifies all deductions and credits available to you
  4. Return Preparation: We prepare your complete return with accuracy and attention to detail
  5. Client Review: We review your return, explain key items, and answer questions
  6. E-Filing: We securely file your return with the IRS and Missouri
