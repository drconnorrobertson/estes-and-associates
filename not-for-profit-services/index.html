Professional Nonprofit Accounting Services

Nonprofit organizations require specialized accounting expertise. Tax-exempt organizations face unique compliance requirements including Form 990 filing requirements, donor accounting, restricted fund tracking, and specific audit standards. Estes & Associates provides comprehensive accounting services tailored to nonprofit needs.

We understand the nonprofit sector and have extensive experience helping tax-exempt organizations maintain accurate records, meet compliance requirements, and demonstrate financial responsibility to donors, boards, and regulatory agencies.

Our Nonprofit Services Include

  • Nonprofit Bookkeeping: Fund accounting and donor designation tracking
  • Form 990 Preparation: Annual tax return filing for tax-exempt organizations
  • Financial Statements: Nonprofit-specific financial reporting and analysis
  • Donor Accounting: Restricted and unrestricted fund management
  • Grant Tracking: Grant compliance and reporting
  • Audit Support: Preparation for nonprofit audits and reviews
  • Tax Compliance: 501(c)(3) and other exempt status requirements
  • Financial Planning: Budget development and financial forecasting
