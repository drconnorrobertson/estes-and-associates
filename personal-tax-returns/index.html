Expert Personal Tax Return Services

Your personal tax situation deserves expert attention. Estes & Associates specializes in preparing comprehensive personal tax returns that maximize your refund while ensuring complete accuracy and compliance with IRS requirements.

Whether you have straightforward W-2 income or a complex financial situation with investments, rental properties, and business income, our experienced team has handled it all. We take the time to understand your complete financial picture and ensure your return reflects every opportunity for tax savings.

We Prepare Returns For

  • Salaried employees with W-2 income
  • Self-employed professionals and business owners
  • Freelancers with multiple income streams
  • Rental property owners and real estate investors
  • Investors with capital gains and dividend income
  • Retirement income recipients
  • Retirees with complex financial situations
  • Married couples filing jointly or separately
  • Single parents with dependent children

Tax Credits We Help You Claim

Tax credits can significantly reduce your tax liability. We ensure you claim every credit you qualify for:

  • Child Tax Credit and Dependent Child Care Credit
  • Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)
  • American Opportunity Tax Credit
  • Lifetime Learning Credit
  • Saver's Credit for retirement contributions
  • Adoption Credit
  • Residential Energy Credits

  • Accurate returns that minimize audit risk
  • Proactive planning to reduce future tax liability
  • Transparent communication throughout the process
  • Local expertise in Missouri tax requirements
