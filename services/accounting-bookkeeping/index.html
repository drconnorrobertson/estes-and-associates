Complete Accounting & Bookkeeping Solutions

Accurate bookkeeping is the foundation of every successful business. Yet many business owners struggle with keeping their books organized, classifying transactions correctly, or finding time for accounting while running their operations. That's where Estes & Associates comes in.

Our accounting and bookkeeping services are designed to give you complete visibility into your business finances while freeing you to focus on growing your business. Whether you need ongoing monthly bookkeeping, quarterly financial review, or year-end accounting support, we have solutions tailored to your needs.

Our Accounting & Bookkeeping Services

Monthly Bookkeeping

We handle all transaction entry, bank reconciliation, and account classification. Each month you receive updated financial statements showing exactly where your business stands financially.

Financial Statement Preparation

Professional balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements that give you clear visibility into your business performance and financial position.

Accounts Payable & Receivable Management

We manage your payables and receivables, ensuring bills are paid on time and invoices are tracked and collected efficiently.

Bank Reconciliation

Every month we reconcile your bank accounts to ensure accuracy and identify any discrepancies quickly.

Expense Tracking & Classification

Proper expense classification is critical for tax deductions and financial reporting. We ensure every transaction is categorized correctly.

Why Professional Bookkeeping Matters

Many business owners try to handle bookkeeping themselves or use a part-time bookkeeper who isn't specialized in their industry. This often leads to:

  • Misclassified transactions that understate tax deductions
  • Inaccurate financial statements that lead to poor business decisions
  • Tax preparation becoming a nightmare because records are disorganized
  • Missed deductions that cost you thousands in taxes
  • Time wasted on accounting that could be spent growing the business

Professional bookkeeping by Estes & Associates prevents all of these problems while giving you accurate, timely financial information to run your business better.

The Estes & Associates Bookkeeping Advantage

  • Industry Experience: We've worked with all types of businesses and understand industry-specific accounting needs
  • Tax-Optimized: We classify transactions to maximize your deductions and minimize tax liability
  • Accurate Financial Reporting: You always have accurate, timely financial statements for decision-making
  • Tax Preparation Ready: When tax time comes, your records are perfectly organized, making tax preparation easier and faster
  • Monthly Visibility: You never wonder how your business is doing financially

What's Included in Our Bookkeeping Service

Our standard monthly bookkeeping service includes:

  • Transaction entry and coding
  • Bank and credit card reconciliation
  • Accounts payable and receivable management
  • Monthly financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow)
  • Payroll processing assistance (if needed)
  • Quarterly review and consultation

Virtual Bookkeeping Available

You don't need to be in the same location as your bookkeeper. We offer virtual bookkeeping services for businesses throughout Missouri. Send us your bank statements, receipts, and transaction data, and we handle the rest. You can access your financial statements anytime online.

Industry-Specific Expertise
