Professional Financial Statement Preparation

Your financial statements are the backbone of business management and decision-making. Yet many business owners don't have accurate, timely financial statements until after their accountant prepares tax returns months after year-end. This delays critical business decisions and makes tax planning impossible.

At Estes & Associates, we prepare professional financial statements that give you real-time visibility into your business finances. Know exactly where you stand, spot trends early, and make informed decisions about your business.

Financial Statements We Prepare

Income Statement (P&L)

Your income statement shows revenue, expenses, and profit or loss for a specific period. We prepare detailed income statements that break out revenue and expense categories so you can see exactly where your money comes from and where it goes.

Balance Sheet

Your balance sheet shows your financial position: assets, liabilities, and equity. This critical statement shows your business's financial health and net worth.

Cash Flow Statement

Many profitable businesses fail because of cash flow problems. Our cash flow statements show where cash comes from and where it goes, helping you avoid cash crunches and plan cash management.

Monthly vs. Annual Financial Statements

We prepare financial statements on whatever schedule works for you:

  • Monthly: Monthly statements give you real-time visibility and enable fast response to business changes
  • Quarterly: Quarterly statements balance frequency with cost
  • Annual: Annual statements provide year-end financial review and tax planning perspective

How to Use Financial Statements

Financial statements aren't just for accountants. Smart business owners use them for:

  • Monitoring Performance: Are we hitting our targets? Are expenses under control?
  • Spotting Trends: Revenue increasing or decreasing? Where is growth happening?
  • Making Decisions: Should we expand? Hire? Invest in equipment?
  • Financing: Banks require accurate financial statements for business loans
  • Tax Planning: Based on year-to-date statements, we can recommend strategies to optimize taxes
  • Business Valuation: Accurate statements establish business value for sale or succession planning

Reviewed vs. Compiled Financial Statements

Depending on your needs and who will use your financial statements, we offer different levels of assurance:

  • Compiled: We compile your financial data into statements. Suitable for internal use and some lending situations
  • Reviewed: We review your statements for reasonableness. More assurance than compiled. Required for some loan situations
