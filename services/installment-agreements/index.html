IRS Installment Agreements: Manageable Payment Plans

Can't pay your full IRS tax bill right away? An installment agreement lets you pay your tax debt over time in manageable monthly payments. It's one of the most straightforward ways to resolve an IRS tax problem.

The IRS offers several types of installment agreements depending on your situation and the amount you owe. We help our clients navigate these options and select the plan that works best.

Types of IRS Installment Agreements

Short-Term Extension

For amounts under $25,000, you can request a short-term extension (typically 120 days) to pay your tax bill without setting up a formal installment agreement.

Streamlined Installment Agreement

For amounts under $25,000, you can set up a streamlined installment agreement with minimal paperwork and no setup fee.

Standard Installment Agreement

For larger amounts, a standard installment agreement requires more detailed financial information but offers flexibility in payment terms.

Guaranteed Installment Agreement

If you owe $25,000 or less and can pay it off within 5 years, you may qualify for a guaranteed installment agreement where the IRS cannot reject your offer.

How Installment Agreements Work

Here's what to expect:

  • Monthly Payments: You agree to make fixed monthly payments for a set period
  • Failure to Pay Penalty: Interest and penalties continue to accrue while you pay, but installment agreements stop wage garnishment and bank levies
  • Setup Fees: The IRS charges setup fees (typically $31-$225 depending on the type)
  • Payment Terms: Payment terms typically extend 24 to 84 months depending on the amount owed
  • Default: If you miss payments, the IRS can default the agreement and resume collection action

Monthly Payment Calculation

The IRS calculates your monthly payment based on:

  • Total amount of tax owed
  • Number of months in the agreement
  • Interest and penalties that will accrue during the payment period

When an Installment Agreement Works Well

  • You have regular income and can sustain monthly payments
  • Your tax debt is moderate (under $50,000)
  • You can get current with the IRS and stay current on future taxes
  • You can pay off the debt within 5-7 years

How We Help

We help with installment agreements by:

  • Analyzing your financial situation to determine the best payment plan
  • Requesting streamlined agreements when possible to minimize paperwork
  • Submitting all required IRS forms and documentation
  • Negotiating payment amounts when possible
  • Monitoring the agreement to ensure you stay in compliance
