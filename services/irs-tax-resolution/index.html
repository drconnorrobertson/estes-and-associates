IRS Tax Resolution: Get Your Life Back

An IRS problem doesn't have to control your life. Whether you owe back taxes, face wage garnishment, liens, or levies, or haven't filed returns in years, Estes & Associates has solutions. With over 30 years of specializing in IRS problem resolution, we've helped hundreds of Missourians get their tax situations under control.

The IRS is like any creditor, except they have more power. They can garnish wages, levy bank accounts, place liens on property, and revoke licenses. But they also have built-in resolution options for taxpayers who take action. We know how to work the system on your behalf.

Common IRS Problems We Solve

Back Taxes & Tax Debt

Owe the IRS thousands or tens of thousands? We have multiple resolution options including payment plans, offers in compromise, and temporary delay of collection while you get your situation organized.

Wage Garnishment

The IRS garnished your wages and you can't afford to live? We can often get the garnishment stopped or reduced through proper IRS procedures.

Bank Levies & Account Freezes

The IRS froze your bank account? We can often get the levy released through immediate action and proper procedures.

Tax Liens on Property

An IRS tax lien on your property prevents you from selling or refinancing. We can get liens removed when your tax situation is resolved.

Unfiled Returns

Haven't filed taxes in years? The IRS will eventually force-file for you, often with penalties. We proactively file unfiled returns and negotiate with the IRS.

Audit Defense

Facing an IRS audit? We represent you before the IRS, gather documentation, and present your case professionally.

IRS Resolution Options We Use

  • Offers in Compromise (OIC): Settle your IRS debt for less than you owe. Requires IRS approval but can provide dramatic relief
  • Installment Agreements: Manageable payment plans that fit your budget while you pay off your tax debt
  • Penalty Abatement: Reduce or eliminate IRS penalties when justified by circumstances
  • Currently Not Collectible Status: Temporarily pause IRS collection action when you're unable to pay, giving you breathing room
  • Innocent Spouse Relief: If you filed jointly but your spouse is responsible for the tax debt, you may qualify for relief

The IRS Resolution Process

Here's what to expect when you work with us:

  1. Initial Consultation: We review your situation, understand what happened, and recommend the best resolution path
  2. Financial Analysis: We gather financial information needed for IRS resolution options like offers in compromise
  3. Documentation Preparation: We prepare all required IRS forms and supporting documentation
  4. Submission & Follow-up: We submit your case to the IRS and handle all follow-up communication
  5. Ongoing Management: Once we reach resolution, we manage the agreed-upon payments or actions

First Step: Stop the Bleeding

If the IRS is actively collecting through garnishment, levies, or liens, our first priority is to stop the collection action. This gives us time to work on a proper resolution. We can often accomplish this quickly once you engage us.
