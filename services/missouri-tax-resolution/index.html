Missouri State Tax Resolution Services

Missouri state tax problems can be just as serious as federal IRS problems, and they're often overlooked until they become urgent. The Missouri Department of Revenue (DOR) has powerful collection tools including wage garnishment, bank levy, and account freezes for unpaid state taxes and licensing issues.

At Estes & Associates, we specialize in resolving Missouri state tax problems and have helped numerous Missourians get their state tax situations under control.

Missouri State Tax Problems We Solve

Unpaid State Income Tax

Owe Missouri income taxes? We work with the Missouri DOR to arrange payment plans or negotiate settlements.

Sales Tax Issues

Business owners sometimes get behind on sales tax obligations. We help resolve back sales tax liability and negotiate with Missouri DOR.

State Tax Liens

Missouri can place a lien on your property for unpaid state taxes. We work to get liens removed once the tax situation is resolved.

HB600 Compliance & License Suspensions

Missouri's HB600 law suspends business licenses for unpaid taxes, child support, and other debts. We help businesses restore licenses and achieve compliance.

State Tax Penalties & Interest

We work with Missouri DOR to reduce or abate penalties and interest when circumstances justify relief.

Understanding Missouri Tax Requirements

Missouri has several tax obligations that business owners need to manage:

  • Missouri Income Tax: Individuals and businesses must file annual Missouri income tax returns
  • Sales Tax: Businesses must collect and remit Missouri sales tax to the Department of Revenue
  • Business License: All Missouri businesses must obtain and maintain a valid business license (subject to HB600 compliance)
  • Estimated Taxes: Businesses with estimated tax liability must make quarterly estimated tax payments

HB600: What You Need to Know

Missouri's HB600 law allows the state to suspend business licenses for unpaid taxes, child support, and other debts. Many business owners discover their license has been suspended without warning, which can shut down operations immediately.

If your business license has been suspended under HB600, we can help you understand what's owed, negotiate with the Missouri Department of Revenue, and get your license restored quickly.

Preventing Missouri State Tax Problems

The best approach is prevention. We work with our business clients to:

  • Ensure timely payment of all Missouri state tax obligations
  • Maintain compliance with HB600 business license requirements
  • Properly estimate and reserve for Missouri tax obligations
  • Plan for tax payments from cash flow

If You're Already in Trouble

If you're behind on Missouri taxes, facing license suspension, or have received notices from the Missouri Department of Revenue, don't ignore them. The situation only gets worse. Contact us immediately and we'll assess your situation and recommend the best resolution path.
