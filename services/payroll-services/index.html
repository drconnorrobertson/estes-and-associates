Complete Payroll Services for Missouri Employers

Payroll processing is time-consuming, complex, and one mistake can cost you significantly in penalties and missed deadlines. Most business owners would rather focus on running their business than wrestling with payroll calculations, tax withholdings, and compliance deadlines.

At Estes & Associates, we handle all aspects of payroll so you can focus on your business. From calculating gross pay and deductions to filing payroll taxes and managing compliance, we've got it covered.

Our Complete Payroll Services

Payroll Processing

We calculate gross pay, apply withholdings, manage deductions, and produce accurate paychecks or direct deposits for all your employees. Available weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly.

Federal Payroll Tax Filing

We file Form 941 quarterly payroll tax returns with the IRS and handle estimated payments. No more worrying about missing deadlines or making calculation errors.

Missouri State Payroll Tax Filing

Complete Missouri state payroll tax compliance including withholding and filing of state taxes.

W-2 & 1099 Preparation

We prepare and file W-2s for all employees and 1099s for contractors with both the IRS and Missouri Department of Revenue.

Employee Withholding Management

We stay current on federal and Missouri withholding tax rates and ensure accurate withholding for each employee.

Payroll Compliance

We ensure your payroll operation is fully compliant with all federal, state, and local payroll requirements.

Why Use Professional Payroll Services?

  • Save Time: Payroll processing can take hours each pay period. Let us handle it while you focus on your business
  • Accuracy: One calculation error can cost your employees money and create compliance issues for you. Professional processing ensures accuracy
  • Compliance: Federal and Missouri payroll requirements are complex and constantly changing. We stay current so you don't have to
  • Avoid Penalties: Missing payroll tax deadlines results in significant penalties. Professional payroll services prevent this
  • Employee Satisfaction: Accurate, timely paychecks keep employees happy
  • Peace of Mind: You know payroll is being handled correctly and all deadlines are met

Payroll Services for All Business Sizes

Whether you have one employee or fifty, Estes & Associates can handle your payroll. Our services scale with your business, so you only pay for what you need.

Integration with Bookkeeping

When you use both our payroll and bookkeeping services, everything works together seamlessly. Payroll data automatically flows into your accounting records, giving you complete financial visibility.
