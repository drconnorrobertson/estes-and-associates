Expert Tax Preparation Services

Tax preparation is more than just filling out forms. It's about understanding your financial situation, identifying opportunities for legitimate tax savings, and ensuring complete compliance with IRS and Missouri tax requirements. At Estes & Associates, our tax preparation services are built on three core principles: accuracy, completeness, and strategic thinking.

With over 30 years of experience preparing taxes for Missouri residents and businesses, we've developed a thorough process that catches details others miss while identifying tax-saving opportunities you might not know about.

Our Tax Preparation Services

Individual Income Tax Preparation

Whether you're an employee, self-employed, or have investment income, we prepare comprehensive individual tax returns that maximize your deductions and credits while ensuring complete accuracy. We handle everything from W-2s and 1099s to capital gains, rental income, and complex financial situations.

Small Business & Corporate Tax Returns

For S-corporations, C-corporations, LLCs, and partnerships, tax preparation is more complex. We prepare complete corporate returns including estimated tax planning, depreciation schedules, and strategic tax decisions that minimize your overall tax burden while keeping you fully compliant.

Estate & Trust Tax Returns

Estate and trust tax returns require specialized knowledge. We prepare Form 706 estate tax returns, Form 709 gift tax returns, and Form 1041 fiduciary income tax returns for estates and trusts with the precision these complex documents demand.

Nonprofit Organization Tax Returns

Nonprofit organizations face unique tax obligations. We prepare Form 990-N, 990-EZ, and Form 990 returns for tax-exempt organizations, ensuring compliance with IRS requirements for tax-exempt status and public disclosure requirements.

Our Tax Preparation Process

We've refined our tax preparation process over three decades to be both thorough and efficient:

  1. Discovery Meeting: We meet with you to understand your situation, review last year's return, and identify any changes or new income sources
  2. Documentation Review: You provide all necessary documents (W-2s, 1099s, receipts, prior tax return, etc.)
  3. Comprehensive Preparation: Our team prepares your complete return, identifying all deductions and credits you're entitled to claim
  4. Accuracy Review: We conduct a thorough review to ensure accuracy, compliance, and optimization
  5. Client Review: We review the completed return with you, explain key items, and answer questions
  6. Filing: We electronically file your return with the IRS and Missouri (if applicable)

Common Tax Deductions We Help You Maximize

Many taxpayers leave money on the table by not claiming deductions they're entitled to. Here are some deductions we help our clients maximize:

  • Self-employed business expenses and home office deductions
  • Mortgage interest and property tax deductions
  • Charitable contributions
  • Education expenses and credits (AOTC, Lifetime Learning)
  • Capital gains and losses from investments
  • Retirement account contributions (IRA, Solo 401k, SEP-IRA)
  • Medical expenses that exceed the threshold
  • Business vehicle expenses and depreciation

Amended Tax Returns

Discovered an error on your filed return? We can prepare amended tax returns (Form 1040-X) to correct errors, claim missed deductions, or claim refunds you're entitled to. Many taxpayers overlook amendments that could result in significant refunds.

What to Bring to Your Tax Preparation Appointment

To prepare for a smooth tax preparation process, bring:

  • Previous year's tax return
  • All W-2s and 1099s
  • Mortgage interest statements (1098)
  • Business income and expense documentation
  • Investment statements and K-1s
  • Charitable contribution records
  • Education expense documentation
  • Medical expense records
  • Estimated tax payments made
