Unfiled Tax Returns: Get Current Now

Haven't filed taxes in years? You're not alone, and you're not without options. Many people end up with unfiled returns for various reasons: job loss, business failure, health issues, or simple procrastination. Whatever the reason, unfiled returns create mounting stress and potential IRS consequences.

The good news: getting current is doable, and often simpler than people think. We help clients file years of back returns, work with the IRS, and get their situation under control.

Why You Shouldn't Ignore Unfiled Returns

Ignoring unfiled returns makes things worse:

  • IRS Will File for You: If you have W-2 income and don't file, the IRS will file a return for you (Substitute for Return or SFR). They'll take no deductions, no credits, and you'll owe maximum taxes
  • Penalties Accrue: Failure-to-file penalties are 5% per month of unpaid taxes (up to 25%). This multiplies with each passing month
  • Interest Compounds: Interest continues to accrue daily on unpaid taxes, compounding annually
  • Collection Action: Eventually the IRS moves to collection action including liens, levies, and garnishment
  • Loss of Refunds: If you're entitled to refunds, the statute of limitations passes and you lose the refund
  • Background Check Issues: Outstanding tax issues can affect employment, housing, and other background check situations

Our Unfiled Return Process

Here's how we get you current:

  1. Initial Assessment: We understand how many years of returns are unfiled and your general tax situation
  2. Documentation Gathering: We gather W-2s, 1099s, or other income documentation for each unfiled year (we can often get copies from the IRS)
  3. Return Preparation: We prepare complete tax returns for each unfiled year, properly claiming all deductions and credits
  4. Analysis: We analyze the returns to understand your total tax liability and what you owe
  5. Filing Strategy: We file the returns strategically to minimize penalties and position you for the best IRS treatment
  6. IRS Negotiation: We work with the IRS to negotiate payment plans or Offers in Compromise if needed

How Far Back Should You File?

You should file all unfiled returns, even if they're old. However, the IRS focus is typically on returns within the last 6 years. We recommend filing at least 6 years back, though we'll discuss your specific situation.

What About Refunds?

Many people with unfiled returns were entitled to refunds but lost them due to the statute of limitations. Good news: if you file within 3 years of the due date, you can claim the refund. We'll identify any refund-eligible years and ensure you claim them.

Will I Have to Pay All the Taxes?

Not necessarily. Here are several possibilities:

  • Refunds Offset Debt: Refund years may offset liability in other years
  • Installment Agreement: You can pay your total liability over time in manageable monthly payments
  • Offer in Compromise: If your situation warrants it, we can negotiate to settle for less
  • Currently Not Collectible: If you're facing genuine hardship, we can temporarily suspend collection action

Common Excuses (Don't Worry, You're Not Alone)

We've heard every reason for unfiled returns, and none of them are disqualifying:

  • Lost my job and didn't have income to file
  • Business failed and I didn't know how to handle it
  • Went through a divorce and everything fell apart
  • Had health issues and couldn't focus on taxes
  • Just procrastinated and it got too big to face
  • Thought I'd be in trouble so I avoided it

Whatever your reason, we can help. The shame should be on the past, not your future. Let's get you current.
