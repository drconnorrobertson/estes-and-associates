Stop IRS Wage Garnishment: Quick Relief

An IRS wage garnishment is devastating. The IRS takes money directly from your paycheck before you see it, sometimes leaving you unable to pay basic living expenses. A typical wage garnishment might take 25% or more of your gross pay, making it nearly impossible to survive.

The good news: we can often get your wage garnishment stopped quickly. Once we engage, we can often place your account in "Currently Not Collectible" status or negotiate an alternative resolution, which stops the garnishment immediately.

How IRS Wage Garnishment Works

The IRS follows a strict process before implementing a wage garnishment:

  1. Assessment of unpaid tax
  2. Demand for payment (Notice and Demand)
  3. Filing of IRS Notice of Federal Tax Lien
  4. Final Notice of Intent to Levy (gives you 30 days)
  5. Wage garnishment begins

How Much Can the IRS Garnish?

The IRS uses a standard deduction formula based on your filing status and pay frequency. For a married person paid weekly, the standard deduction is substantial (around $1,500), but anything above that is subject to garnishment. The exact amount varies by personal circumstances.

How to Stop a Wage Garnishment

There are several ways to stop an IRS wage garnishment:

Option 1: Currently Not Collectible Status

We request that the IRS place your account in "Currently Not Collectible" status, which temporarily stops all collection action including garnishment. This is appropriate when you're experiencing genuine financial hardship and cannot sustain payments. The IRS may file a lien but suspends garnishment, giving you breathing room.

Option 2: Installment Agreement

We negotiate an installment agreement where you pay the IRS a manageable monthly amount, which stops the garnishment. You stay in control of how the IRS gets paid.

Option 3: Offer in Compromise

If your situation warrants it, we negotiate an Offer in Compromise to settle the debt for less than owed. Once the offer is accepted, the garnishment stops.

Option 4: Challenge the Garnishment

In some cases, we can challenge the validity of the garnishment through the IRS appeals process or Tax Court.

Timeline to Relief

The timeline to relief depends on which option we pursue:

  • Currently Not Collectible: Relief within 1-2 weeks in many cases
  • Streamlined Installment Agreement: Relief within 2-3 weeks
  • Standard Installment Agreement: Relief within 4-6 weeks
  • Offer in Compromise: Relief typically 2-4 months once submitted

What We Need from You

To help stop your garnishment, we need:

  • Your IRS notice(s)
  • Recent pay stubs
  • Bank statements
  • List of living expenses
  • Information about other debts
  • Willingness to work with us on a resolution

Act Now

If you're facing an IRS wage garnishment, don't wait. Contact us immediately. The longer you wait, the more damage the garnishment does to your finances. We can often stop it quickly.
