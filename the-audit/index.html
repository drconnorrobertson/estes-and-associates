Professional Audit Services & Preparation

An IRS audit can be stressful and time-consuming. Estes & Associates provides comprehensive audit preparation and representation services to help you navigate the audit process successfully. We handle the details so you can focus on your business.

Whether you've received an audit notice or want to prepare proactively, our experienced team guides you through every step of the audit process. We represent your interests with the IRS and work to achieve the best possible outcome.

Our Audit Services

  • Audit Preparation: Organizing documents and preparing responses
  • IRS Correspondence: Responding to audit letters and requests
  • IRS Meetings: Representing you in meetings with IRS agents
  • Documentation: Preparing supporting documentation for audit items
  • Dispute Resolution: Resolving disagreements with IRS findings
  • Appeals: Representing you in the appeals process if needed
  • Settlement Negotiation: Negotiating favorable settlements
  • Future Compliance: Recommendations to reduce future audit risk
